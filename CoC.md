# KSDC Code of Conduct

The Kola Saami Documentation Corpus (KSDC) includes data with different access conditions. Some data are openly available, while access to other data is restricted because of copyright or other considerations.

By collaborating in the KSDC project or receiving access to restricted KSDC data, I agree to the following:

* I will use KSDC data only for research, teaching, or other purposes agreed upon with the KSDC administrators.
* I will respect the copyright, licensing, and access conditions applying to the individual parts of KSDC.
* I will not publish, distribute, upload, or otherwise share data from **KSDC-fair** or **KSDC-bound** outside the group of authorised users without permission from the KSDC administrators.
* I will not give other persons access to the private KSDC repositories or make copies of restricted data available to them.
* When publishing results based on KSDC data, I will appropriately acknowledge KSDC and the original sources, as described in the [KSDC README](README.md).
* If I am uncertain whether particular data may be used, quoted, published, or shared, I will consult the KSDC administrators before doing so.
